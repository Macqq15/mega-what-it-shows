How to tell them apart

  • parser: day and month swapped (“Booked for 2 March. The invite says 3 February.”). A day-first tenant, any channel except a booking-v3 form. The shared parser reads month first.
  • hint: the same swap, but only from booking-v3 forms at day-first tenants. The form prints MM/DD/YYYY as its hint, people type what it says, and the form reads it day first. The swap happens in the form, so a parser fix does not reach it.
  • move: no swap at all. The date moves by one day, differs by where the attendee is (Lisbon Tuesday, Toronto Monday), or the invite is stamped before the booking. That is a time-zone fault. The desk read these by hand (oneDayMove in the desk notes) and then filed them under parser by tenant.
